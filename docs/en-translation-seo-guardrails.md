## Policy: 1 English search intent = 1 primary English target URL
- New English articles must be complete translations/adaptations, not Vietnamese bodies with English navigation.
- Existing EN /goth-vs-grunge-style.html = **comparison of Goth vs Grunge**. A translation of grunge-vs-punk targets **Grunge vs Punk**, not a second Goth vs Grunge comparison.
- Existing EN /how-to-style-gothic-graphic-tees.html = **gothic graphic tee outfit styling**. Translation of Vietnamese generic tee styling must remain **general T-shirt styling for women in alternative fashion**, with no duplicate Gothic graphic tee intent.
- Existing Vietnamese /blog/goth.html is a **Goth topic hub**; Gothic overview and subtype articles each need a clearly distinct search intent when translated.
- The `collision_risk` column is a screening hint only. Cannibalization is confirmed by overlapping **query × page** performance in GSC, not by shared words in slugs.
- Do not create /en/ stub pages, mass index autogenerated English text, 301 redirect old Vietnamese URLs, or set canonical of EN page to VI page.

## Per-article publication gate
1. Inspect full original source and current GSC queries (when available).
2. Pick unique English primary intent and final English slug; compare against all existing EN pages.
3. Translate full body, image captions, data tables, examples and relevant references; localize naturally and preserve core facts.
4. QA English title, meta, H1/H2, images and alt, working links; keep Vietnamese original untouched except adding English alternate/hreflang and a visible EN switch.
5. English and Vietnamese self-canonical; reciprocal `hreflang=en` and `hreflang=vi`. Only add when the EN page actually exists.
6. Add published EN URL to sitemap and link from English Journal. Check mobile rendering, crawlability and page quality.

## Manual overlap review candidates based on Vietnamese titles
The inventory records source titles and groups to review. A shared head term alone **does not mean cannibalization**:
- `quan-cargo-la-gi.html` (definition) vs. `quan-cargo-nu-mua-o-dau.html` (shopping) have different purposes.
- `phoi-do-voi-quan-ong-loe-nu.html` (flared trousers) vs. `quan-ong-rong-nu-phoi-voi-ao-gi.html` (wide-leg trousers) concern different silhouettes.
- `dark-feminine.html` (topic hub) vs. `dark-feminine-outfit-phoi-do.html` (practical outfit guide) need different primary intent and links.
- `phoi-do-voi-ao-thun-nu.html` (general women's tee styling) vs. existing English gothic graphic tee guide requires semantic differentiation.

**Do not merge, redirect, or noindex either page solely due to title overlap.** A confirmed cannibalization diagnosis requires Search Console export joining **query, page, country, date** over a consistent date range, supplemented by actual content and search results. VI/EN language pairs should be treated separately in that analysis.

PRIVACY POLICY & WEBSITE TERMS

English translation prepared November 5, 2026. The Vietnamese source policy states its last update as August 2026. Read the Vietnamese policy.

Apocalypse Closet ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used and protected when you use apocalypsecloset.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or email, we collect your display name, email address and profile picture where supplied. This information supports your account and personalized features. Google Analytics may collect information about website usage, including page visits and interaction patterns, according to the active configuration.

2. How We Use Information

Information supports the personal Closet feature, improvements to the website and support when needed. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Storage and Synchronization

Sign-in is handled through Google's Firebase Authentication. Outfits saved to Closet are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your signed-in account so they can be synchronized when you use the same account on another browser or device.

The website may use localStorage to retain basic account-interface information, such as your display name, profile picture and user ID. LocalStorage is not the primary storage for your saved Closet outfits.

4. Shopee Affiliate Links and External Shopping

Some Shopee links are affiliate links. Shopee or its affiliate network may record referral URLs, device information, cookies and clicks to attribute a commission. Apocalypse Closet may receive a commission on qualifying purchases without increasing the price you pay.

Payment, delivery, returns, warranty and payment information for Shopee orders are handled by Shopee or the seller. Apocalypse Closet does not collect or store your card or payment-account details.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The website uses cookies or related storage for features such as sign-in. Google Analytics may be used to understand traffic and usage according to the active configuration.

6. Your Choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated Closet data by emailing apocalypsecloset@gmail.com. You can also remove individual saved outfits in Closet.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on the policy page with its updated date.

8. Website Terms

Use the website lawfully. Do not attempt unauthorized access to another person's account, data or systems, or disrupt website operation.

Outfit content and product suggestions provide general information. Prices, availability, descriptions and sales policies may change on Shopee. Purchases take place directly between you and Shopee or the relevant seller.

Access to account features may be suspended where misuse, fraud or a security risk is identified. Continued website use after terms are updated is treated by the source policy as acceptance of the updated version.

9. Contact

For privacy or website-term questions, email apocalypsecloset@gmail.com.

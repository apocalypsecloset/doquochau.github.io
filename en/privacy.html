PRIVACY POLICY & WEBSITE TERMS

Updated November 20, 2026. Both language versions describe the same practices.

Tiếng Việt

Operator and contact

Apocalypse Closet operates this website’s fashion editorial, Closet features and presentation of original POD designs. Send questions or requests about data we handle to apocalypsecloset@gmail.com. This policy covers our website; Fourthwall orders have separate policies.

Information processed

When you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication handles account information; the website may use your display name, email, profile image and user ID. Cloud Firestore synchronizes saved outfits. Support messages contain what you send. If you allow analytics, GA4 measures page views and selected interactions. This technical data is not described as wholly anonymous. Do not enter sensitive information in search fields.

Purposes and providers

Data supports accounts, saved outfits, the shopping bag, support and site security; optional analytics helps assess use of our content. Google/Firebase provides authentication, storage and analytics. Cloudflare provides content delivery, security and the shop API route. Providers may receive IP addresses, device information and data needed for their service. See Google Privacy and Cloudflare Privacy.

Cookies and analytics choices

Necessary storage supports sign-in, saved outfits, the cart ID and cookie choices. GA4 is not loaded and analytics events are not sent before you allow analytics. “Necessary only” still permits reading and shopping. Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of a page to change your choice; withdrawing after GA4 has loaded reloads the page to stop analytics. Global Privacy Control keeps analytics off. This website does not request advertising storage or ad personalization.

Fourthwall orders

Fourthwall is the seller and merchant of record for POD orders in our Fourthwall store. It handles checkout and order data; we may receive customer/order details for shop management and support. Card details are entered at Fourthwall checkout. See its Merchandise Privacy Policy. Our cookie choice does not control external websites.

Retention and international processing

Cookie choices expire after 180 days. Analytics cookies created by this configuration have a maximum 180-day lifetime without renewal on every view. Account/Closet data is retained to serve the account until deleted or a deletion request is processed; support retention depends on resolving the request and applicable obligations. Provider-side retention also depends on service configuration. Providers may process data in multiple countries; we do not claim all data stays in Vietnam.

Your controls and requests

You can remove saved outfits in Closet, sign out and change cookie choices. Email us to request account-data access, correction or deletion; we need to verify the requester. Where applicable law provides rights to object, restrict processing, obtain/transfer data or complain to an authority, identify that right in your request. Withdrawal does not erase information already processed; request deletion separately where needed. For Fourthwall transaction requests use Fourthwall order support.

Children and updates

This website is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child provided data so we can review deletion. Anyone below the applicable age to purchase independently needs a guardian. Changes and the update date will be published here.

Terms and shopping policies

Website terms are published separately at Terms. Read Shipping and Returns & refunds for POD orders. These pages do not exclude mandatory rights under applicable law.

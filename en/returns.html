POD Returns & Refunds

Updated November 16, 2026. Both language versions describe the same practices.

Tiếng Việt

Misprints or damaged products

Under the order provider’s policy, contact support within 30 days of delivery for product defects. Provide the order number and clear, well-lit photos of the problem. The order provider reviews replacement or refund under the applicable terms. Do not ship an item back without instructions. See its quality-issue guidance.

Change of mind, sizing and order changes

The standard made-to-order policy excludes general change-of-mind and sizing returns, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Check measurements before ordering. For changes/cancellation, use the confirmation-email link and contact support promptly; changes are not guaranteed once production starts.

Statutory withdrawal rights

EU, EEA and UK consumers may withdraw from eligible goods within 14 days of delivery, or the last item in a split order. Made-to-order status alone does not remove this right. Conditions and exceptions are in the Merchandise Terms. The order provider handles requests through order support. This page does not limit mandatory rights.

Refund handling

The order provider and its payment provider process approved refunds. Posting time depends on the payment method/bank; follow your order notification rather than assuming an instant credit. This policy concerns orders from our Shop, not Shopee purchases.

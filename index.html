Before & after delivery

Each tee is made to order. Check your size and the checkout estimate before paying. Follow the care label supplied with your tee.

For a print error or damage, send your order number and clear photos to order support within 30 days of delivery. Requests are reviewed under the applicable terms.

The standard made-to-order return policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights. Read Returns & refunds and Shipping before ordering.

How do I choose a size?

Compare a tee you own, laid flat, with our garment size chart. These tees have a relaxed fit. The chart measures the garment, not your body; use the measuring tips before ordering.

What is the Silent Chaos tee made from?

The product listing specifies 100% ring-spun cotton, heavyweight 6.1 oz fabric and a relaxed fit with garment-dyed softness. Silent Chaos features original gothic angel artwork on the front.

Where can you ship, and when will my order arrive?

Destination availability and delivery estimates are shown at secure checkout. Production happens before transit, and timing depends on your item and location. Check the estimate before paying; it is not a guaranteed arrival date. Read shipping details.

Are shipping and taxes included in the displayed price?

Displayed prices are in USD and cover the product. Shipping and applicable taxes are calculated at checkout. Review the full total and any import-fee notice before paying. See shipping and charges.

Can I return a tee if I choose the wrong size?

The standard made-to-order policy excludes general change-of-mind and sizing returns, subject to mandatory consumer rights. Compare measurements before buying. Eligible statutory withdrawal rights and exceptions are explained in the returns policy.

What if my tee has a print error or arrives damaged?

Contact order support within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear, well-lit photos. The order provider reviews replacement or refund under the applicable terms. Do not ship an item back without instructions. Read the full policy.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Use the link in your confirmation email and contact order support promptly. Changes or cancellation are not guaranteed once production starts. Read order-change details.

How does secure checkout work?

Apocalypse Closet creates the original artwork; our order provider handles production, payment and order support. Your bag continues to secure checkout. Use your order confirmation for tracking and support.

How should I wash and care for my tee?

Follow the garment care label supplied with your tee. If care instructions are unclear, contact order support with your order number before washing.
